Privacy Policy

How this independent plumbing information and referral website may collect, use, and share information when you browse or call.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

San Diego Plumbing Guide is an independently operated informational and lead-generation website about plumbing services in San Diego. The website operator does not itself perform plumbing work.

This policy describes information that may be collected, used, and shared when you browse the website or telephone the number displayed here.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the services actually enabled and your interactions, we may process information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, general plumbing concern, location, and scheduling preferences. Telephone service providers may make caller ID, phone numbers, call times, and routing details available.

Web hosting systems normally process basic technical information, such as IP address, browser details, and request times, to serve and protect pages. The current website does not include a contact form or user accounts. Do not provide payment details or other sensitive information to this informational website.

3. How We Use Information

Information available to the website operator or its service providers may be used to respond to inquiries, route callers to potentially relevant independent plumbing providers, support requested consultations, maintain the website, evaluate inquiry quality, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website is intended to generate inquiries about plumbing services. Once a working telephone referral line is connected, calls may be directed or referred to independent third-party plumbing businesses. The website operator may be paid for qualifying telephone inquiries or referrals.

Independent plumbing providers control their own services, prices, appointments, and privacy practices. Information you share directly with a provider is subject to that provider’s practices. Calling does not automatically authorize unrelated marketing calls, texts, or messages.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

If enabled, third-party telecommunications services may route or track calls and process call numbers, duration, connection details, and related information needed to facilitate inquiries.

Call recording has not been confirmed as active. If recording is introduced, appropriate notice and any consent required by law must be provided before calls are recorded.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

This version of the website does not include a dedicated analytics tag, advertising pixel, or cookie-consent tool. Basic hosting logs may still be processed to operate and protect the site. If additional cookies or analytics services are introduced, this policy should be updated to identify the relevant practices.

7. Information Sharing

Information from a call may be shared with the independent plumbing provider to whom an inquiry is routed or referred. Information may also be processed by the hosting and telecommunications vendors necessary to operate the website or phone service, if configured.

Information may also be disclosed where legally required or reasonably necessary to prevent misuse. We do not claim that inquiry details are never shared, because referral is a purpose of this site.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information processed for the website. No internet or telecommunications system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention depends on the actual hosting, call-routing, and provider arrangements and applicable obligations. No fixed retention period has been established for this website.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. The operator must provide a verified contact method and appropriate process before launch to receive privacy requests.

To limit the information you share, do not disclose personal details during a call unless needed for the inquiry. Independent providers may have separate processes for information they collect directly.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Independent service providers and external websites may have separate policies, terms, and information practices. Please review those policies when communicating directly with a business or visiting its website.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, telephone service, service-provider relationships, or legal requirements change. The latest version will appear here.
